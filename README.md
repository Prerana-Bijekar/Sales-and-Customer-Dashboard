**Sales & Customer Dashboard**

📌 **Project Overview:**
- This project is an interactive business intelligence solution built using Tableau to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, profit trends, and business growth through dynamic visualizations and filters.
- The dashboard enables users to:
    - Monitor key business KPIs 
    - Perform year-over-year performance analysis 
    - Analyze customer purchasing patterns 
    - Explore sales and profitability trends interactively 
- It helps stakeholders make data-driven decisions by transforming raw business data into meaningful visual insights.

🎯 **Objectives:**
-	Analyze sales and profit performance
-	Compare current year performance with previous year
-	Monitor customer behavior and order patterns
-	Identify profitable customers and product categories
-	Enable dynamic filtering and dashboard interactivity

📂 **Dataset Information:**

**Data Modeling:**
- Fact Table:
    - Orders
- Dimension Tables:
    - Customers
    - Product
    - Location

🧭 **Dashboard Pages & Features:**

**1️. Sales Dashboard:**
- KPI overview for Sales, Profit, and Quantity
- Year-over-Year sales performance analysis
- Weekly and monthly sales trend monitoring
- **Visuals Included:**
    - KPI Sales (Line Chart)
    - KPI Profit (Line Chart)
    - KPI Quantity (Line Chart)
    - Subcategory Comparison (Bar-in-Bar Chart)
    - Weekly Trends (Line Chart)

**2. Customer Dashboard:**
- Customer growth and order analysis
- Sales per customer tracking
- Top customer profitability insights
- **Visuals Included:**
    - KPI Customers (Line Chart)
    - KPI Sales Per Customer (Line Chart)
    - KPI Orders (Line Chart)
    - Customer Distribution (Bar Chart)
    - Top Customers (Table)

📈 **Key Insights:**

**1. Sales Dashboard Insights:**
- Overall sales increased by 20.4% in 2023 compared to 2022, indicating strong business growth.
- Total profit reached $93K with a 12.5% increase, showing improved profitability over the previous year.
- Quantity sold increased by 26.8%, reflecting higher customer demand and order volume.
- Phones, Chairs, and Binders were among the top-performing subcategories in terms of sales contribution.
- Certain subcategories such as Tables and Machines generated losses, indicating areas that require pricing or cost optimization.
- Weekly sales and profit trends showed several above-average performance periods, especially in the later months of the year.

**2. Customer Dashboard Insights:**
- The total number of customers increased to 693, showing positive customer growth in 2023.
- Total orders reached 1,687 with a 28.3% increase, indicating higher customer engagement and purchasing activity.
- Average sales per customer increased by 10.8%, reflecting improved customer spending behavior.
- Most customers placed only 1–3 orders, while only a small percentage were repeat high-frequency buyers.
- Top 10 customers contributed a significant portion of overall profit, highlighting the importance of key customer relationships.
- Customer purchasing trends fluctuated throughout the year, showing seasonal variations in order patterns and sales activity.
